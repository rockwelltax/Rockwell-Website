How the Process Works

From first question to preparing and filing your return—every step is transparent and secure.

  1. 1

    Client Inquiry

    Tell us a little about your tax needs through our simple public questionnaire. Please do NOT enter Social Security numbers or upload tax documents here.

  2. 2

    Price Confirmation

    Rockwell reviews your answers and emails you the confirmed fee before any work begins. You are not charged until you agree to the quote.

  3. 3

    Pay Upfront

    Pay securely using the hosted payment link sent after your fee confirmation. Straightforward and transparent.

  4. 4

    Secure Upload

    Upload your tax papers through our separate, approved secure portal sent after fee approval. Never through public web forms.

  5. 5

    Prepare & Review

    Rockwell prepares your return and reviews it thoroughly with you before filing. Nothing is submitted until you have reviewed and approved it.

Compliance & Trust

Important Notes for Tax Filers

No guaranteed outcomes. Rockwell prepares returns carefully and accurately following tax guidelines, but does not guarantee a specific refund amount or tax outcome.
Your documents are secure. Tax documents are shared through a separately approved secure portal only—never through this website's public forms.
Professional Practice Standards. Our tax preparation services are led by an experienced tax return preparer with an active IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN). For tax and accounting firms, back-office preparation support is coordinated under the direction and review of the firm’s practitioner of record.
