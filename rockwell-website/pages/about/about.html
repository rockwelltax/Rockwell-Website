About Rockwell Tax & Digital Strategies

Rockwell Tax & Digital Strategies Inc. was founded by Fritzie Ann Silva to help individuals and small business owners feel more confident about taxes and day-to-day business tasks.

Fritzie has hands-on experience preparing tax returns, helping clients gather information, organizing financial and administrative records, and improving the way work gets done. She holds a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and a master's degree in Database Technologies. Her background also includes years of work supporting teams, technology systems and business operations.

At Rockwell, that mix of tax, business and technology experience means we focus on staying organized, explaining things clearly and paying attention to the details that matter to you.
